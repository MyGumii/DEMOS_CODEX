# P2.4 graph, finding, and labelled-history importer evidence

Date: 2026-08-13 (Asia/Seoul)

## Result

P2.4 passes its isolated reviewed-plan-bound graph/finding/history boundary.
The production apply service accounts for every reviewed P2.4 import effect,
accepts only supported valid same-personal-space relationships, and commits each
canonical effect with an immutable checkpoint, audit event, and outbox event.

This evidence does **not** claim that a production snapshot was migrated. It
does not claim P2.5 conversation/operational-state import, P2.6 observed
verification/rebuild, P2.7 fault/large migration scenarios, P2.8 rehearsal,
any `MIG-001..008` completion, traffic cutover, worker startup, or enabled
personal/part reads or writes.

## Frozen implementation boundary

- Physical schema v5 preserves v1-v4 and adds unique finding/audit targets plus
  an immutable P2.4 relationship ledger. Existing P2.3 mappings remain exact;
  update/delete attempts and duplicate relationship identities fail closed.
- Graph nodes become dependency checkpoints only after their reviewed source
  item mapping resolves in the same personal space. Valid graph edges become
  deterministic canonical edges. Evidence-backed pending candidates become
  canonical findings, and exact hidden/source duplicates become one durable
  provenance relationship. Ambiguous, orphan, cross-owner, cross-space, or
  unsupported relationships are never promoted by P2.4.
- Each transaction revalidates the reviewed user's current role/status/part and
  active personal space, then commits effect, record/relationship checkpoint,
  audit, and outbox together. Graph/candidate/legacy-edge containers close only
  after their exact reviewed child and relationship set exists.
- Personal wiki logs and tombstone files are retained as content-addressed raw
  historical artifacts. Valid tombstone/audit JSONL children are recomputed
  from the exact parent bytes with duplicate-key rejection and stored as
  hash-only historical audit events. Raw global auth-audit bytes remain in the
  frozen source snapshot; neither their container nor child events are current
  authorization, publication, or operational state.
- Source reads, JSONL parsing, and content-addressed staging happen outside
  SQLite; retained handles bind staged bytes through the service-owned commit.
  Batches are sorted, unique, no larger than 100 effects or 256 MiB, and are
  preceded/followed by storage and snapshot-high-water verification.
- Exact replay inserts zero new mappings or effects. A committed prefix resumes
  from immutable checkpoints. Failpoints after the canonical effect or after
  the checkpoint roll back effect, checkpoint, audit, and outbox together.

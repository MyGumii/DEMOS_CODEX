- Publication now exposes every documented transaction and response-loss seam,

- Account, membership, lease, edit, lifecycle, merge, curation, proposal,
  migration, backup, projection, and durable-job paths retain their exact

  failpoints.
- Transaction-internal injected errors roll back the exact canonical baseline.
  Post-commit errors, client response loss, and hard process termination are
  retried with the same operation/effect identity and create no duplicate
  canonical effect.

## Projection and process recovery

- Projector failpoints cover durable claim, all staged writes, Markdown/index/
  graph replacement, and the boundary immediately before outbox
  acknowledgement.
- Injected disk-full, permission, and atomic generation-replace failures never
  publish a false manifest. The next attempt converges to the canonical
  generation.
- A failed attempt may leave only an exact
  `.generation-<32 lowercase hexadecimal>` directory. Recovery removes that
  abandoned staging tree through the pinned generations root before rebuild.
  Other names, symlinks, and reparse entries are not cleanup candidates.
- A real child process killed after generation replacement but before ack
  replays without duplicate state. Incremental settlement and full rebuild
  produce the same checksum, and canonical drift during staging never exposes
  a mixed generation.

## Durable jobs, operations, and backup

- Enqueue, claim, domain-commit-before-ack, and ack boundaries are independently
  injected. Claim loss recovers after lease expiry; post-domain-commit loss
  returns the stable result; submitted jobs minus terminal/recoverable jobs is
  zero.
- The fixed seed is `0x5077`. It runs exactly 20 sequential child-process hard
  terminations: ten immediately after claim and ten during handler execution.
  Every job converges to `succeeded`; lost jobs and duplicate outbox/domain
  effects are zero.
- Separate processes prove conflicting GPU work cannot execute independently.
  Account disable or membership movement is rejected by the final authority
  check before the domain effect commits.
- Online backup during concurrent writes passes SQLite integrity and manifest
  verification. Backup holds prevent the blob-GC race, and manifest/finalize
  failures do not leave a falsely completed backup.
- Isolated restore/rebuild, runtime/SQLite preflight, actual Waitress service
  composition, named stop, and in-flight reverse drain satisfy
  `OPS-001..006`. This remains isolated execution, not a signed target restore.

## Safety and deliberate limits

- All mutation, filesystem-failure, process-kill, backup, and restore tests use
  disposable paths beneath `D:\CodexTestTemp`. They do not inspect or modify
  live-looking compatibility roots.
- No test starts the production service wrapper or durable worker as an
  operator deployment. Process tests use isolated child processes, random
  loopback ports where needed, and disposable databases.
- P7.7 proves deterministic recovery and exactly-once canonical effects at the
  documented fault seams. It does not measure target hardware capacity,
  forty-client latency, final busy rate, target RPO/RTO, or target service
  authority.
